02 / Bring your own footage. Import a video or record a screen using your browser’s picker. Add focus points on the timeline and draw redactions where needed.

03 / Capture the clicks. The optional Chrome extension records an active tab plus pointer motion and click locations. Stop capture to open this studio with automatic zoom points.

04 / Take it with you. Export a silent WebM, a README GIF, or a PNG cover. Save a project to keep your source footage and edits together.

Video support depends on your browser. MP4/WebM generally work in current Chromium; MOV depends on its codec. Imported clips are limited to 80 MB and 10 minutes. Exports run in the foreground; keep this tab visible. This release does not capture audio, synthesize narration, or automatically remove pauses.

Show it once. Keep the clicks.

The optional Chrome extension captures your active tab and records pointer motion and clicks. Stop recording to open a local editor with automatic focus points.

  1. Download and extract the extension.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and choose Load unpacked.
  3. Select the extracted folder. Open your app and click the RepoReel toolbar icon to start.
  4. Perform your demo, then open the icon again and stop capture.
Download extension ↗

Chrome 116+. No account or API key. Captures only after you start it. Cross-origin frames and browser-internal pages are not supported. The extension is distributed as source; it is not a Chrome Web Store listing.
